Most AI competitive intelligence tools suffer from total amnesia.

You run a web crawl, get a snapshot of today's pricing, and tomorrow the agent has completely forgotten everything it saw.

Stateless AI can tell you what your rival charges today. It cannot tell you what changed since last month, why they changed it, or what strategy they are building toward.

To solve this, we built God's Eye 👁️ — an autonomous Competitive Intelligence Agent powered by Vectorize Hindsight persistent memory.

Here is how persistent memory changes the game:
1. retain(): Automatically logs competitor pricing events, feature launches, and 7-day news signals with real temporal anchors.
2. recall(): Pulls historical facts across prior scans using multi-strategy search.
3. reflect(): Native cross-temporal trajectory synthesis that compares past baselines with today's moves and prescribes tactical counter-measures.

The 3-Question Framework:
• What happened? (Verified facts, competitor profiles, pricing matrix)
• Why does it matter? (Macro trends, opportunity gaps, threat detection via Hindsight)
• What should we do? (Prioritized P0/P1 executive counter-measures)

Stop building stateless AI that forgets. Persistent memory is the real frontier of agentic intelligence.
